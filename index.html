Project Details

Business Problem & Objectives

Insurance teams need a clear view of their policy portfolio and claim patterns. This project brings multiple categories together to support exploratory analysis and reporting.

Business Objectives

  • Analyze policy distribution by car usage and manufacturer.
  • Compare policies across customer age groups.
  • Explore coverage zones and vehicle-year trends.
  • Review claim amounts by education and marital status.

Dashboard Features

  • KPI cards for high-level monitoring.
  • Charts for category comparisons and trends.
  • Policy distribution by customer and vehicle attributes.
  • Visual exploration of insurance claim patterns.

Insights & Limitations

Business Value

The dashboard helps users compare policy distribution and explore claim amounts across available customer and vehicle categories, identifying areas that may need further analysis.

Observed patterns alone do not establish causation or prove insurance risk. Validate findings against the source data before using them for underwriting or pricing decisions.
